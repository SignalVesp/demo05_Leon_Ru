Non-Standard Pathways

Eternal Aeon Pathway

A Pathway that represents a predetermined fate, it specializes in ritualistic dances and spells, accumulating strength and gaining powers from other beings. They excel at manipulating and foreseeing fates.

Chaos Primogenitor Pathway

A Pathway that mixes the power of Reality and Procreation of The World Pathway and the power of Evil of the Chaos Primogenitor. It specializes in floral and alchemical knowledge, heretical magic, creation of new life, and more.

Patriarch Pathway

A Pathway that mixes the power of reasoning of the Desires Pathway and the power of madness of the Blathering Pathway. It specializes in inducing desires, imitating people and powers through acting, life draining, and more.

Condenser Pathawy

A Pathway revolving around the usage of fundamental forces while possessing astronomical knowledge of the Stars. It specializes in manipulating gravity, electromagnetic forces, capturing powers from celestial bodies and creating cosmic voids.
